not another chatbot

It doesn't just answer. It does.

ShortKut is an agent with hands: give it a job in plain language and it works your computer the way you would.

Files & folders

Reads, writes, renames, and organizes — locked inside one workspace folder you choose.

The terminal

Runs shell commands, installs apps with Homebrew, scripts anything scriptable.

Your apps

Opens and drives real apps — WhatsApp, Notes, Spotify, your browser — clicking and typing like a person.

It can see

Reads the screen with your computer's built-in OCR — works even with text-only models, and screenshots are deleted the moment a task ends.

Your people

Finds contacts, sends WhatsApp messages, starts calls — with the recipient verified against your address book first.

Live & visible

While it works, a purple glow frames your screen and a crayon halo rides your cursor — you always see it working.

you hold the crayon

Fast when you trust it.
Careful when it matters.

Approve every action — or none.

Commands, deletions, and automations ask first. Flip on Auto mode and ShortKut runs free, with a Stop button always one click away.

Messages can't go rogue.

Recipients are verified against your Contacts, first-time recipients always need your OK, and duplicate sends get flagged — even in Auto mode.

Never your secrets.

ShortKut refuses to type passwords, one-time codes, or payment details. Those stay yours.

private by design

There is no ShortKut server.

Nothing to sign up for, nothing to leak. The only network traffic is between your machine and the model provider you chose.

Keys stay locked

API keys are encrypted by your Mac's Keychain and never leave the device.

Chats are files

Conversations live as local JSON on your disk. Delete a chat, it's gone.

Screens stay yours

Screenshots are temporary, sent only to your chosen model, and scrubbed the moment a task finishes.

ShortKut — made with a purple crayon. Your computer, your models, your keys.
